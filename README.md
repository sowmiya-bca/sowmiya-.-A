## Project Theme

**PocketSmart AI – Your Smart Budget & Recommendation Assistant**

PocketSmart AI is an intelligent personal finance assistant that helps users manage their income and expenses. It analyzes financial information and provides personalized budgeting and recommendation support.

## Project Objectives

* To help users track and understand their income and expenses.
* To create personalized budgets based on the user's financial information.
* To provide smart recommendations for better money management.
* To help users identify unnecessary spending.
* To make personal financial planning simple and easy to understand.
* To use AI to provide personalized financial assistance.

## Problem Statement

Many people find it difficult to manage their income and expenses effectively. They may not know how much to spend, save, or allocate to different needs. PocketSmart AI aims to provide a simple AI-based solution that helps users understand their spending and make better budgeting decisions.

## Key Features

* **Income & Expense Tracking** – Record and manage financial transactions.
* **Smart Budget Planning** – Create a budget based on income and spending patterns.
* **AI Recommendations** – Provide personalized suggestions for managing expenses.
* **Expense Analysis** – Understand spending patterns and categories.
* **Personalized Assistance** – Give recommendations according to the user's financial information.
* **Simple Interface** – Make financial planning easy for users.

## Expected Outcome

PocketSmart AI will provide users with a simple and intelligent way to manage their personal finances, plan budgets, understand spending habits, and receive personalized recommendations.

## Technology

* **Backend:** Python, FastAPI

## Project Goal

The main goal of PocketSmart AI is to make personal budgeting easier by combining financial tracking, budget planning, and AI-powered recommendations in one application.
